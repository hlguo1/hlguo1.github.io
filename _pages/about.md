I build **reliable AI/ML systems** — systems whose outputs can be trusted where the stakes are real.

That single problem connects everything I do. In **scientific discovery**, a model that hallucinates a material or misses a defect wastes months in the lab. In **business operations**, a dashboard that invents a number costs a small shop real money. In **frontier LLMs**, post-training data that cannot be measured or verified cannot reliably improve coding or science. My work is about closing that gap — grounding AI in verifiable data, measuring its quality systematically, and auditing what it produces — whether the system serves a researcher, a seller, or millions of Meta AI users.

I am a Software Engineer at [Meta Platforms, Inc.](https://www.meta.com/) in Menlo Park, California, working on **Meta AI** post-training, including contributions to flagship models **Muse Spark 1.1, 1.2, 1.3** and **Muse Glimmer**. I received my B.A. in Computer Science from [Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu/).

## Research Agenda: Making AI Verifiable at Every Scale

- **Reliable post-training for LLMs.** Recursive self-improvement pipelines for synthetic data generation, RL data generation for AI for Science (AI4S), and evaluation frameworks that systematically measure coding capability and data quality — so model improvements are earned and measurable, not assumed.
- **Reliable ML for scientific discovery.** Physics-constrained and measurement-grounded models for materials discovery, energy systems, and manufacturing — from welding-defect vision systems to laser–materials processing — where every claim must survive contact with the physical world.
- **Reliable AI for business operations.** Deterministic-first platforms where every dollar cites its source, every action is auditable, and nothing runs on phantom tasks — built for small businesses that cannot afford a data team or a wrong number.
