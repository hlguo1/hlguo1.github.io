**[Omni-One](https://github.com/hlguo1/omni-one)** — the business thread of my reliability thesis: an enterprise-grade proactive AI platform (82 stars, 10 forks, 6 watchers) for shops that cannot afford a wrong number.

- The 5-minute Daily Close for 1–5 person Shopify / Etsy / Amazon shops: true profit, reorder sheet with PO draft, angry-customer reply queue, leak detection, and accountant-ready exports — in one offline, $0 command where every dollar cites `file:line`.
- Same design language as my science and Meta work — verifiable data, measured quality, auditable output: Foundry (versioned datasets, DuckDB) → Ontology (typed business twin) → 4-layer pipeline → 12 seller scenarios → Workshop → AIP (grounded checks) → Governance (hash-chained audit with `verify()`).
- Scale: 100k+ lines of code, 870+ files, 1,350+ tests, 10 channel connectors, 5-locale i18n, TS + Go + generated Python/TS/Go clients, 17 MCP tools for Claude/Cursor, REST API, and Close + Accountant PWAs.
- Adoption by small and medium-sized e-commerce businesses.

Related talk: "Omni: A Data Synthesis Platform for LLMs," Data Science Institute, Columbia University, 2025.
